You know, [color=blues]Blues[/color] doesn't like drama, especially when it forces me to choose sides.
In Voxel World, Blocky and Cartoon residents may sometimes have disagreements, but they always try to find a way to communicate and solve their problems.
However, on social media, it is not uncommon to see people **blocking each other over unexpected reasons.** Sometimes, people may even expect others to take a side simply because they have a connection with someone involved.
But that's not how I want to build my community.
As my principle states, **I won't involve myself in personal dramas, and I won't take sides.** If necessary, I will keep my distance from everyone involved.
**My focus will always remain on creating, sharing, and welcoming people who enjoy this world.**
