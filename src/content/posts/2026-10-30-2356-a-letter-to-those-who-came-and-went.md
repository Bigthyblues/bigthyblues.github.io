Firstly, **thank you all for visiting Voxel World!** I am glad my drawings have made you smile!
Well, I've recently adjusted my following list. Here's why I may have unfollowed you.
Some of you** I followed for a long time, but you never followed back**, even when we seemed to get along. Eventually, I stopped waiting.
Some of you followed me back at first, **then quietly unfollowed me. **I didn't notice for a while and kept following you. **Once I found out, I decided to unfollow you too.**
**It's okay for people to come and go.** But my time and attention are limited, and I want to **spend them on people who genuinely value our connection.**
My doodle page is still open to everyone. You’re welcome to look around, but **I won’t chase anyone anymore.**
To those who left: **take care and enjoy your own life.** I’ll be here with my close friends.
