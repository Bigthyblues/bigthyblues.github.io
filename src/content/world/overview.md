# Voxel World

![Voxel galaxy](/img/image-world/World1.png)

**Pixels are the fundamental building blocks of every world.**

Among countless creations across different dimensions, **Voxel World **is a remarkable place where different forms of life, artistic styles, and cultures can meet and create new stories together.

## Welcome to Voxel World

![Voxel preview](/img/image-world/World2.png)

**Voxel World** is a beautiful **square-shaped **planet in the Blocky dimension.

Almost everything here is built around **simple geometric forms**. Square buildings, unusual inventions, creative homes, and pixel-based designs can be found throughout the world.

Voxel World was originally inhabited mainly by **[color=fazer]Blocky[/color] residents.** As more **[color=rowdy]Cartoon[/color] residents** arrived from other dimensions, they brought new ideas, lifestyles, and forms of creativity, **making the community more diverse than ever.**

## Life in Voxel World

![Voxel city preview](/img/image-world/World1.1.png)

Voxel World is **a technologically advanced place** filled with convenient facilities, unusual inventions, and homes with many different designs.

Some inventions are practical and improve everyday life, while others are **strange, playful, or created simply for fun.**

From busy city districts and peaceful towns to wide natural landscapes, every resident can find a place that suits them.

Life here is not only about technology or exploration. Residents spend their time creating inventions, cooking and enjoying food, listening to music, visiting friends, running businesses, traveling, or simply relaxing together.

Because of its comfortable environment, creative freedom, and welcoming community,** Voxel World has become a beloved home for both [color=fazer]Blocky[/color] and [color=rowdy]Cartoon[/color] residents.**

## Different Styles, One Community

![Blocky and Cartoon](/img/image-world/World3.png)

Voxel World welcomes residents **with different appearances, designs, and artistic styles.**

[color=fazer]Blocky [/color]and[color=rowdy] Cartoon[/color] residents may follow different physical rules and experience the world in different ways,** but both groups are important parts of the community.**

### [color=fazer]Blocky[/color]

[color=fazer]Blocky[/color] residents are the native inhabitants of Voxel World and are deeply connected to its system.

Most of them have **square or rectangular bodies**, although their shapes, colors, and features can vary greatly.

Their appearance is not restricted to a fixed age or life stage. Through** the "Create a Blocky" system**, they can redesign or update their appearance without losing their identity.

[color=fazer]Blocky[/color] residents can also **summon a personal interface in front of them. **This interface allows them to **manage items, check their condition, organize information, and access different functions connected to the world.**

Because of their deep connection to the system, [color=fazer]Blocky[/color] residents can naturally** generate [hex=#ffe835]Pixels[/hex] through positive emotions and meaningful experiences.**

### [color=rowdy]Cartoon[/color]

[color=rowdy]Cartoon[/color] residents are characters who have **moved to Voxel World** from other dimensions.

Unlike [color=fazer]Blocky[/color] residents, they are **not fully connected to the world's system.** Because of this, certain functions and parts of daily life may be more limited or less convenient for them.

However, [color=rowdy]Cartoon[/color] residents can **bring their own creativity, skills, cultures, and personalities** into the community.

**They cannot naturally generate Pixels in the same way as [color=fazer]Blocky[/color] residents**, but they can earn them by **working, exchange with their money**, or receiving **tips and support from [color=fazer]Blocky[/color] residents**.

Their arrival has helped Voxel World grow **beyond its original Blocky community and become a shared home for many different artistic styles.**

## The Pixel Economy

![Pixel economy](/img/image-world/World4.png)

[hex=#ffe835]Pixel [/hex]is the primary currency of Voxel World.

For [color=fazer]Blocky[/color] residents, [hex=#ffe835]Pixels[/hex] are **closely connected to positive emotions, enjoyable experiences, and meaningful social connections.**

[color=rowdy]Cartoon[/color] residents and other newcomers earn [hex=#ffe835]Pixels [/hex]**through work and community participation rather than generating them automatically.**

[hex=#ffe835]Pixels [/hex]are used for** everyday purchases, services, entertainment, travel, and many other parts of life.**

More than just a currency, [hex=#ffe835]Pixels[/hex] also represent the happiness, creativity, and connections created throughout the community.

## Exploring Voxel World

![Voxel world map](/img/image-world/Voxelmap.png)

Voxel World is **much larger than a single city. **It contains many regions with different environments, lifestyles, and attractions.

### [hex=#044aff]City Area[/hex]

**[hex=#044aff]The city[/hex]** is the modern heart of Voxel World.

It contains **advanced technology, workplaces, shopping centers, entertainment venues, restaurants, apartments, and busy gathering places.**

Many residents come here to work, shop, meet friends, or experience the latest inventions. **And there's always filled with many tourists every day.**

### [hex=#ffd986]Town Area[/hex]

**[hex=#ffd986]The town area[/hex]** offers a quieter and more relaxed way of life.

It is filled with **smaller houses, local shops, peaceful streets, and friendly neighborhoods. **Many residents choose to live here because it feels comfortable and personal while still remaining connected to the city. **They can also build their dream home with their creativity and love!**

### [hex=#ff675c]Amuse Area[/hex]

**[hex=#ff675c]The Amuse area[/hex]** is a great place for entertainment, filled with residents and visitors every year.

It contains **large sport stadium, funland and various entertainment facilities.** Many people come here to watch exciting contests, enjoy amusement rides, and spending time with their friends. **There are always exciting championships and events here that attract a lot of people!**

### [hex=#66ff2c]Nature Areas[/hex]

Beyond the city and towns are many **[hex=#66ff2c]natural regions[/hex] waiting to be explored.**

These include **beaches, forests, deserts, snowy landscapes, flower fields, and other unusual environments.**

Some residents visit these places for** outdoor activities, fishing, surfing, hiking, or adventure. Others simply come to enjoy the scenery and take a break from city life.** Sometimes they can discover beautiful places to recommend to their friends!

## Dimensional Travel

![Dimensional Travel](/img/image-world/World5.png)

Visitors and residents can travel between dimensions through **approved portals and compatible devices.**

They start their travel with **portal doors** at **Traveller Hub.** Not every dimension follows the same artistic or physical rules. Some characters may need to **adjust their appearance or art style** when entering another world, while others may keep their original form.

Voxel World is designed to **accommodate different forms of life**, allowing[color=fazer] Blocky[/color] and[color=rowdy] Cartoon[/color] residents to meet without completely losing the visual identities of their home dimensions.

## A Safe and Welcoming Community

![Background review](/img/image-world/World5.1.png)

Voxel World has **a strict security system** designed to protect its residents.

Visitors arriving from other dimensions must enter through approved portals and **complete a background review** before they are allowed to stay.

The system evaluates whether a visitor may pose a serious risk to residents or the community.

Characters who** seriously violate the rules** may be [color=fizz]removed [/color]from Voxel World and **prevented from returning for an extended period.**

These rules are intended to **keep the community safe, rather than unfairly target [color=rowdy]Cartoon [/color]residents or any particular group. **The same basic standards apply to everyone, although certain procedures may differ because [color=fazer]Blocky[/color] and [color=rowdy]Cartoon [/color]residents are connected to the system in different ways.

## Living Together

![Living together](/img/image-world/World6.png)

[color=fazer]Blocky[/color] and [color=rowdy]Cartoon[/color] residents usually live together peacefully, but **differences in values, habits, communication styles, and daily routines** can sometimes lead to misunderstandings.

Their bodies and system access are also different, so one rule may not always affect both groups in exactly the same way.

Voxel World therefore aims to** maintain shared standards **for everyone while making reasonable adjustments for the different needs of [color=fazer]Blocky[/color] and [color=rowdy]Cartoon [/color]residents.

**Conflicts may still happen sometimes**, but residents are encouraged to **solve them through communication, fairness, patience, and cooperation.**

## Growing Together

![Growing together](/img/image-world/World7.png)

**Every resident brings something special to Voxel World.**

Some create inventions. Some open shops, prepare food, perform music, explore nature, or help newcomers find their place in the community.

Others simply bring happiness by spending time with their friends.

Voxel World is not only a place made from pixels.** It is a shared home shaped by the people who live there and the experiences they create together.**

As [color=fazer]Blocky[/color] and [color=rowdy]Cartoon[/color] residents continue to learn from one another, **Voxel World will keep growing into a brighter, more creative, and more welcoming place for everyone!**
