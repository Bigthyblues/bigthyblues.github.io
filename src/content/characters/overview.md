# Character profiles

Meet the residents of **Voxel World** - a place where Blocky and Cartoon characters live together, sharing countless fun moments, exciting adventures, and unforgettable stories!

Explore each **character's profile, reference sheet, and personal journey** to discover the unique personalities that make this world special!

## [color=fazer]Blockimals[/color]

[color=fazer]Blockimals[/color] are the native residents of Voxel World.

They usually have **square or rectangular bodies**, with designs inspired by simple blocky shapes. Despite their geometric appearance, they can still **move, stretch, and react with the lively energy of classic cartoons.**

Through Voxel World's creation system, they may also **update or redesign their appearance while keeping their identity. **Meet the [color=fazer]Blockimals[/color] who live, work, explore, and create new stories across Voxel World!

## [color=rowdy]Cartoons[/color]

[color=rowdy]Cartoons[/color] are characters who have moved to Voxel World from other dimensions.

Each of them brings **a different art style, personality, culture, and way of life into the community.**

Although they are **not as deeply connected to Voxel World's system** as [color=fazer]Blockimals[/color], their creativity and enthusiasm **make the world warmer, livelier, and full of unexpected moments.** Meet the [color=rowdy]Cartoons[/color] residents who have chosen Voxel World as their new home!
