# Zesty

> Wanna see me surfing

## Profile

We are ready for surfing!

Oh, let me introduce myself first here...

Zesty is a Cartoon-style cat living in Voxel World, easily recognized by his tropical outfit and the blue bell collar he always wears.

He’s a little on the short side, but full of energy and charm.

With his strong swimming skills and a fearless heart, Zesty is always heading off to the beach for his next big adventure.

Not just for fun, but to find something special and remember these great moments.

He moves so quickly and loves the feel of the sea scene.

When he’s bored, you’ll often find him swimming, surfing, or fishing there—and if he catches a big fish, he’s happy to give random strangers a crash course in fish facts. (Or invite them to enjoy his roast fish!) As an expert surfer, Zesty can pull off just about any trick on the waves, and he does it with his expert style.

But he can only do this well with his own surfing board.

However, he really dislikes stormy weather, since it means no beach time, and he’s also not great at handling being ignored in conversation —he thrives on openness and connection.

Zesty loves being out in nature and often invites friends to join him outdoors.

Whether it’s to unwind, enjoy the scenery, he believes everything’s better with our company. (We are out just for the FREE fish he cooked!)
