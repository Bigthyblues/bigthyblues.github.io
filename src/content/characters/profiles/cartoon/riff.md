# Riff

> Let's fill the world with rock music!

## Profile

Here's the melody lover badger, Riff!

Let's rock on! 🦡💜 is a Cartoon-style badger living in Voxel World, with a tall and slender body and a purple bandana decorated with a white music note. (Music is always with me!) He is a passionate guitar player who loves performing whenever he feels bored or wants to bring more energy to the atmosphere.

In his original dimension, Riff often received complaints from his neighbors and could not play as freely as he wanted.

Because of this, he moved to Voxel World, where he could finally enjoy making music in a more welcoming place. (Haha, I can play my guitar as I want!) Riff is ambitious, brave, and confident.

He enjoys composing songs in his studio and sharing his rock music with everyone around him.

He also loves chatting with his friends and fans.

When he feels lonely, listening to music always helps him feel better.

Although Riff is popular, troublemakers can still get on his nerves.

He may also feel a little uncomfortable when someone else’s music becomes more popular than his.

He also dislikes messy rooms, especially when they make it harder for him to find his instruments.

With his passion of rock music, and strong connections with his fans, Riff hopes to become the best rock musician in Voxel World and fill it with cheerful melodies!
