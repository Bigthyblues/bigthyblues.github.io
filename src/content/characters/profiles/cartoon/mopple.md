# Mopple

> It's a sweet and magical time!

## Profile

It's a sweet and magical time!!!

Let's have fun together here!

Mopple is a Cartoon-style frosted apple candy cat living in Voxel World.

He moved here and began a new life as the shopkeeper of his magical dessert shop, determined to achieve his ambition: becoming the best dessert maker in the universe! (You must love it!) He is tall and a little fat, with a creamy hat and a magical creamy tail.

Has an appearance like a frosted apple candy.

He'd wear different shirts and outfits depending on the seasons and events.

He runs a magical dessert shop, offering affordable and delicious treats with enchanting effects, all made with love.

His deep passion for spreading love to his friends drives him to help them with warmth and kindness.

But he hates it when troublemakers sabotage his hard work and make him feel sad.

He can be a bit silly and funny at times, occasionally causing trouble with his clumsy tail and spilling too much sour cream all over the street.

He only knows how to make desserts and can't cook anything else.

Furthermore, he is also very scared of water because it might make him wet and upset. (Yeah, I can't swim because I don't like being wet!) He lives in a candy house with a candy yard, and it’s truly beautiful.

Only his friends are allowed to visit, but we can learn how he creates his magical desserts.

During Christmas, the yard opens up for everyone to enjoy. (Free to visit, just don't touch my candy plants!) As a special cat with a warm heart, kindness, and a touch of silliness, as well as skillful dessert- making, he is our cool new cartoon friend in Voxel World! Mopple  Bigthyblues
