# Mish Jr.

> Let's do something fun!

## Profile

Oh, it's the first time I saw you! Hi! I am Mish Jr., Mish's little cousin. 🐱💛

Mish Jr. is a very young cartoon kitten living in Voxel World along with his older cousin Mish. He's short and slim and usually wears his signature blue overalls. (They look cool!)

He moved to Voxel World after Mish moved to there for a while and live with Mish ever since. Mish takes good care of his little cousin, though keeping an eye on such an energetic kitten isn't always easy! And usually, Mish Jr. likes go out and play with Glup, and new places across the city. (We are good friends together!)

Mish Jr. is naughty, and cheerful, full of curiosity. He is too naughty sometimes when he finds something fun to do, even its not allowed to do. And Glup will always try to stop it before getting into big trouble. 

He likes snacks too, especially grilled sausages or fried sausages, and enjoys gaming with Mish and his friends. When he's free, he will discover the blocky world along with his friend Glup. There's always somewhere new to visit!

Like many kids, he doesn't like being lectured or told he can't do something fun. When he's in somewhere boring, he will probably try to leave as soon as possible.

There are more things he will discover with Glup someday. When adults are busy with their boing work, it's the best time for him to go out and play!
