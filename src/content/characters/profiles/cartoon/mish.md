# Mish

> This is the paper eyes I drew for you!

## Profile

Wanna see my paper eyes

Mish is a Cartoon-style cat living in Voxel World.

After receiving a special reboot makeover from Blues, he now has a much cooler and awesome appearance.

He is tall and thin, he wears a purple necktie and a doodle paper eye —a piece of paper with hand-drawn eyes in different art styles.

He was born blind, and this magical item is actually the only way he can see the world. (But I can see an artistic world and I love it so much!) Although he appears to be an office worker, he prefers spending time playing around, exploring Voxel World rather than sitting behind an office desk.

He’s got a great sense of humor, never gets angry, and is always willing to help everyone.

With his kindness and laid-back vibe, he has earned a reputation as a gentleman and a funny person. (I often get a free office meal with my tie!) At his apartment, Mish enjoys making Japanese food, especially a simple favorite: rice with raw egg for dinner.

He also loves doing doodles in his free time.

He often invites Blues over to try his homemade meals or to chat about drawing tips —two things he deeply enjoys. (My dinner is so tasty!) As a cat with a magical paper eye and a calm, joyful personality, Mish has a unique charm that many people appreciate! Mish  Bigthyblues
