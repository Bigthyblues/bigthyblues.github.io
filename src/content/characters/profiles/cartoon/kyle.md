# Kyle

> Take my hand and let's fly!

## Profile

Hey bro! I’m Kyle, the flying guy! Take my hand and let’s go!

🐺🤍 is a white Cartoon-style fox-wolf living in Voxel World, with a tall, slender, and fluffy body and a blue-and-yellow propeller backpack he always wears. (Time to fly!) Kyle originally lived in a toony dimension.

After Blues gave him his propeller backpack, he quickly fell in love with flying and began exploring his world from the sky.

As Voxel World continued to grow, Kyle visited the city from time to time.

After thinking about it for a long while, he finally decided to settle down and stay there with his old friend Blues. (It’s good to be with my best old friend!) Kyle is silly, cheerful, and full of positive energy.

He enjoys flying around, exploring unfamiliar places, and chatting with friends along the way.

He is also fascinated by trains and often travels by himself to see different ones.

Thanks to his propeller backpack, he can visit many distant places with ease.

Kyle dislikes anyone touching or playing with his propeller without permission.

He also hates unnecessary drama and becomes upset when others mock or bully him because of his backpack.

With his joyful spirit and love of exploration, Kyle is ready to bring even more fun to Voxel World—and his newest adventure has only just begun! Kyle  Bigthyblues
