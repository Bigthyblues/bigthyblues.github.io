# Rushy

> Are you interested in my square glasses

## Profile

Hey!

This is a sport cat, Rushy here! Let's go!

Rushy is a Cartoon-style cat living in Voxel World, also a close friend of Mish.

He’s full of energy—bold, lively, and a bit dramatic.

With his square glasses and a green bandana, he totally looks like the kind of cat who loves driving around in a car with the windows down.

Though Rushy was born nearsighted, that never stopped him from chasing adventure.

Thanks to his determination and the square glasses, he’s become incredibly athletic! (He wore that for many years, just changed the lenses a couple of times!) He loves joking around with others, and sometimes his playful pranks go a bit too far.

Even when he tries to make things right, he often ends up getting into more mischief... (But it’s all part of his charm.) He's brave when something goes wrong.

He will be the first to face them and try to solve them. (Like...

I apprehended a drunk guy at a party) Rushy is a big fan of parties and pizza, and always ends up being the life of the party —the one everyone notices first.

With his unstoppable spirit and the special glasses, Rushy dreams of breaking all kinds of records in Voxel World! (And honestly

We really admire him for that!)
