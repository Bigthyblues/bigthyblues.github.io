# Verde

> You really should visit Voxel World!

## Profile

Hiii! Long time no see!

I am Verde the green fox, I am back!

🦊💚 is a Cartoon-style fox living in Voxel World, with a tall and slender body and a red bandana he always wears. (I’m proud of it!) He was the first Cartoon character invited to live in Voxel World.

After seeing the city’s potential, Verde decided to settle down and become part of its growing community.

He had so much fun exploring Voxel World that he began introducing it to others, encouraging more and more Cartoon characters to visit. (You should come and see it too!) Verde is kind, cheerful, and a little silly.

He enjoys discovering new places, traveling around Voxel World, and spending time in nature.

He especially loves fishing with his friend Wisky, and sharing jokes around his friends.

And take a bite of blocky donuts would be the best thing for him!

Although Verde is usually positive and friendly, he can become angry when things go badly.

He also dislikes loud noises and cannot stand being mocked or humiliated.

Verde cares deeply about his friends, so losing someone close to him can be especially painful.

As one of the earliest Cartoon residents and a witness to Voxel World’s growth, Verde hopes to keep exploring with his friends and help the world become an even brighter place!
