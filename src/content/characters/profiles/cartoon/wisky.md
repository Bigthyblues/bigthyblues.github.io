# Wisky

> Coffee, quiet walks, and good company.

## Profile

Oh, hello there, this is Wisky, nice to meet you!

🦊🧡 is a Cartoon-style fox living in Voxel World, with a tall and slender body and a blue bow tie he always wears. (It makes me look gentle, right

He enjoys taking quiet walks through the city, visiting coffee shops, and relaxing in the central park near his home. (Have you even see blocky birds in the park

His calm personality often makes his friends feel comfortable and welcome.

He loves fresh coffee, enjoys telling jokes, and often travels around Voxel World with Verde.

As someone who values peace and order, Wisky dislikes messy places, loud noises, unnecessary drama, and arguments with rude people.

With his warm personality and gentlemanly nature, Wisky enjoys spending time with his friends and hopes to bring more comfort to the Voxel World community!
