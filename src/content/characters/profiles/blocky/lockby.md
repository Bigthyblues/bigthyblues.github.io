# Lockby

> Big candy, big fun!

## Profile

Hi!

I am just a normal blocky guy, wearing headphones, you can call me Lockby!

Here's more you may like to know! 🤍 Lockby is a humorous resident of Voxel World, he's in white and black like doodle guy, with square-shaped body, only wearing headphones. (I love them very much!) He once lived in the Doodle World and worked as an actor in a pencil show.

After losing his role and being treated poorly by his coworkers, he ended up alone, homeless, and hungry.

Blues later found him, invited him to live in Voxel World, and gave him his unique headphones.

After that, Lockby decided to become stronger.

He works out every day, not only to protect himself, but also to help his friends when they are in trouble.

Lockby is a humorous, brave but a bit naughty guy, he loves to try something new.

He loves hanging out with Carl, a new friend who's caring.

He loves doodle something with a small pencil in his pocket, and he will be happy if he get a big lollipops! (Big candy, big fun!) Since keeping healthy is what he usually does every day, which means he really dislikes being sick.

Of course, because of what happened to him during his work as an actor, he would be very upset when he or his friends get poor treatment.

He's so simple but full of energy, he is more a friendly blocky guy, and we all love him.

His strong body and the funny moments would be always a great point of him!
