# Blues

> Hmmm, I love this song!

## Profile

Woof! I am Blues!

A blocky dog from Voxel World!

🐶💙 Blues is a resident of Voxel World, with the appearance of a blocky dog and a few other unique traits.

He has a square-shaped body and is often hanging out and about, going on walks, enjoying good food, or rushing to the mall to grab the latest music albums (I can get them every time!).

One of his most iconic features is the pair of headphones he always wears.

They're not just for listening to music—they're also a part of his signature outfit.

He also uses his rocket shoes to fly around, adding an extra boost to his everyday life.

Blues is best friends with Fizz, and the two spend nearly every day together, often diving into new adventures.

As characters who can shift between different art styles, they also enjoy traveling to other cartoon dimensions and exploring new worlds.

Calm and composed by nature, Blues is still filled with energy and warmth.

He’s incredibly friendly toward his friends —but oddly enough, he tends to be harsh when dealing with glitchy computers. (I can be calmer when encountering a technical issue for now!) As someone skilled in using a magical music box, Blues brings joy to those around him and often helps solve problems in creative ways.

In order to travel across those cartoon dimensions, Blues has so many art styles to choose from and change when arriving in a new dimension! (And sometimes I'd keep blocky, because I am too lazy to draw a complicated art style love that art style very much!!) Blues  Bigthyblues
