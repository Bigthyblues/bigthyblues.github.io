# Fizz

> I am ready to fly!

## Profile

Oh, hey!

I am Blues ' best friend, Fizz!

Ablocky cat from Voxel World! Wanna know more about me

🐱❤ Fizz is a cheerful resident of Voxel World, with the playful appearance of a cat and a blocky, square-shaped body.

For him, flying isn't just a way to travel—it’s part of his daily routine and something he loves. (And I often fly out from the window of my apartment, then check whether Blues is awake or not!) His unique huge propeller backpack is his only decoration, but it plays a big role in his life.

It has unlimited power, also light for him, and it features a temperature adjustment pad on its back, which allows him to feel warm or cool.

His mind controls it, so he can decide when he can fly.

And when he is excited or scared, the propeller will automatically spin!

Thanks to it, Fizz is often the first customer to arrive at the store, especially when there’s something new and exciting for him to grab! (I always wear my propeller all the time, though I often get stuck at the door...) He loves drinking some fizzy soft drinks, most of which are sugar-free.

Fizz is optimistic, energetic, and loves to talk—sometimes for hours—especially when he's sharing food and conversations with his best friend, Blues.

He likes Blues so much that he often launches into a joyful flying hug the moment they meet.

When he's out and about, Fizz enjoys exploring the world from the sky, snapping fun photos, and collecting something interesting from his travels around Voxel World. Fizz  Bigthyblues
