# Dex

> This is so valuable, isn't it?

## Profile

The Voxel World's #1 collector, Dex, is here for you! Wanna have a check at your collections? 🦝💙

Dex is a resident of Voxel World, with a rectangular raccoon body and his signature tie. He works as a collector and trader, dealing with all kinds of interesting collectibles for both Blocky natives and Cartoon travelers. (I am goood at it, yeah!)

His love for collecting began when he was young, and then he becomes a true collector. He has collected many interesting things from anywhere or bought from someone.  They are mostly stored in his home, and something he likes were displayed in his collection shop, where he also buys, sells, and trades collectibles.(Hope you have fun in my shop!)

Dex is a kind also a confident raccoon! He likes collecting something unusual or interesting. His tie is an important part of his look, and he often changes its pattern depending on his mood. He enjoys using what he got to DIY something different, making his friends amazed. As a gamer, he also has strong interest in retro games and collecting their consoles and cartridge.

Trading with Dex is always welcome! His careful eyes also makes him especially good at spotting and exposing fakes, earning him the trust of others and helping him maintain his reputation as Voxel World's #1 collector.

As a collector, messy space is not suitable to display things and he doesn't like it. He will be mad too when one of his collections is missing! His tie is so significant that he will always wear it and don't want to lose it.

With his knowledge, sharp eye, and careful approach to collecting and trading, Dex is a great person in collections and trading. If you want to see something cool, go find him!
