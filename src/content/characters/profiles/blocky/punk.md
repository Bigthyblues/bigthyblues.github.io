# Punk

> Three eyes and one very long tail, ready to play!

## Profile

Yo, what’s up

I’m Punk, a cheerful blocky cat living in Voxel World! Let’s go!

🐱💛 is a resident of Voxel World, with a square-shaped cat body, three eyes, a cap, and an unusually long tail. (It's so looooog!) He got his special tail after a strange arcade accident.

While trying to play a game for free, he stuck his tail into the coin slot and received an electric shock.

After that, he discovered that his tail could extend and retract freely!

Punk loves using his tail to grab objects, reach distant places, and create funny moments. (That’s way more useful than paying for the game!) Punk is a cheerful, playful guy; he really enjoys outdoor time with Blues and Lockby.

He is also a huge fan of arcade games, and his three eyes give him an impressive advantage while playing.

He also often invites his friends to hang out at Voxel Mall.

At home, he also uses his flexible tail to help him build and craft interesting things.

Like many cats, Punk dislikes sudden loud noises.

He also becomes frustrated when his tail causes trouble, especially when someone grabs or plays with it without permission.

With his three eyes, amazing tail, and love for gaming, Punk is one of the funniest and most unique residents of Voxel World!
