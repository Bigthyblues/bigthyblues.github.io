## Profile

Oh, hi!

I am a tall blocky guy, named Carl, a gentleman and nice helper in Voxel World!

Here, let me introduce myself! 💜 Carl is a resident of Voxel World, with a tall purple body, a green tie, and a floating yellow hat. (Floaty would look better) He also works at an advanced IT company in Voxel World.

Though he has more free time, he usually enjoys coffee in a quiet place when he has nothing to do.

And he likes DIY in his work room, which made his room filled of interesting inventions! (Hmm... a self-heating coffee mug sounds useful!) Carl is polite, patient, and always willing to help his friends.

He is especially skilled at crafting and often uses his inventions to solve everyday problems.

He also enjoys having thoughtful conversations and sharing useful ideas with the people around him.

As a gentleman, Carl dislikes messy places and loud noises that disturb his peaceful breaks.

He also becomes deeply upset when someone betrays his trust or takes his kindness for granted.

Thanks to his gentle and caring personality, Carl soon became close friends with Lockby.

With his patience, creativity, and helpful nature, Carl makes the Voxel World community a warmer and better place!
