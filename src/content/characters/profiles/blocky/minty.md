# Minty

> No ingredients, no cooking!

## Profile

This your most beloved private chef, a blocky rat, from Voxel World, named Minty! 🐭🩶 is a cheerful resident of Voxel World, known for his blocky rat appearance and a few unique quirks.

He has a square-shaped body, with blocky big ears.

Likes cooking something delicious, collecting rare cheese, and through different places to discover some new ingredients. (Hey!

I found a plant that smells like cheese!) He usually only wears hiking shoes, making it easier for him to explore and travel far.

He also has his very own chef hat and a spatula, and a huge collection of kitchen tools and seasonings.

He will switch into his chef outfit when someone is hungry, and ready to cook!

He often trades with Fizz to collect special cheese (and yes… he usually eats them all afterward).

He also love teaching some recipes and sharing cooking tips with his friends.

He can be hired as a private chef, but you need to buy all the ingredients first! (Because he simply can’t cook properly when the ingredients aren’t enough!) Minty has a strong passion for cooking, he's gentle and calm in most time, he’s gentle, calm, friendly, and kind—especially toward his friends.

But he doesn't like it when he saw someone waste food, he’ll walk right up and tell them to stop… or he might jokingly threaten to “eat them up instead.” He will always excited when his food makes everyong happy. Minty  Bigthyblues
