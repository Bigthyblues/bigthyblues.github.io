# Zippy

> Ready to skate, and definitely ready to win!

## Profile

Heyo!

I’m Zippy, a blocky dinosaur living in Voxel World!

Oh, is this our first time meeting

Nice to meet you! is a resident of Voxel World, he's a green blocky dinosaur, wearing blue headphones and a pair of red sneakers. (Always ready to move!) He is a huge fan of skateboarding and loves riding through the streets while listening to hip-hop and rock music.

When he gets hungry, Zippy heads straight to a nearby fast-food restaurant for hamburgers, pizza, and anything else that gives him energy.

Zippy also enjoys entering skateboard contests and often performs very well. (And the prize money usually becomes burger money!) Zippy is lively, playful, kind, and a little naughty.

His skateboard tricks sometimes cause small accidents, but he never means to seriously hurt anyone.

He also likes eats fast food when he thinks he need some energy.

If someone asks him where to eat, fast food will almost always be his first choice.

Although he acts confident, Zippy is quite afraid of scary things.

He also takes skateboard competitions very seriously and absolutely hates losing a match.

Even though he can be mischievous and occasionally causes trouble, Zippy’s energy, ambition, and cheerful spirit always help brighten up Voxel World!
