## How the blocky style began

My blocky style was partly inspired by a cranky doodle monster I once saw in classic Pencilmation. I liked the idea of simple doodle creatures wearing different outfits and becoming their own little characters.

From there, the style slowly grew into something more personal. These characters already looked a little blocky, so I kept exploring that shape and giving them more personalities, stories, and worlds to live in.

Most of my artwork pairs blocky characters with cartoon friends from many different worlds. I am less interested in realism than in characters that are easy to recognize, fun to draw, and full of imagination.

The style may be simple, but it lets these little blocky guys do almost anything.

## Why I made this site

I've always loved the idea of having a website of my own, so one day I bought a domain and decided to build one.

It began as a place to organize my artwork and introduce my original characters. As it grew, I added Posts for updates, artwork, and little stories shared at my own pace.

Here, you can explore my work without distractions or the fast pace of social media. Everything has a home together, and I can keep building this little world without worrying too much about algorithms, likes, or follower counts.

This website is not just a gallery anymore. It has become the main home for my artwork, original characters, and Voxel World.

## My creative rhythm

I usually draw in the afternoon or around midnight, after I have taken care of real-life things.

Nighttime is especially nice because more of my friends are online, so I can draw and chat with them at the same time.

## The future of Voxel World

Voxel World is my original universe. It is home to blocky characters who welcome visitors from many different cartoon dimensions.

Everyone brings their own habits, personalities, and cultures, so everyday life often becomes an adventure filled with funny misunderstandings, unexpected friendships, and new stories.

Many of my original characters live there, and this website is where their world continues to grow.

I hope to build out the whole of Voxel World and turn it into a real series called **Blocky Tales**!
